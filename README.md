## 📌 About the Internship

The **Electric Vehicle Modeling and Simulation Design Virtual Internship Program** was designed to develop practical knowledge and technical understanding of electric vehicle systems, modeling, simulation, and performance analysis.

The internship followed a structured learning approach combining conceptual learning, practical simulation activities, hands-on exercises, assignments, quizzes, and assessments. The program covered important concepts related to electric vehicle architecture, electric powertrains, battery systems, electric motors, power electronics, controllers, vehicle dynamics, and system performance evaluation.

The internship provided practical exposure to simulation-based approaches used for analyzing and evaluating different electric vehicle components and systems. It also helped bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical applications in modern electric mobility technologies.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

The general learning and simulation architecture followed during the internship consisted of the following layers:

### 1. Vehicle Architecture Layer

Defines the overall structure of the electric vehicle, including the battery, electric motor, power electronics, controller, and other major components.

### 2. Battery Layer

Represents the battery energy-storage system and its characteristics, including charging, discharging, voltage, current, and energy availability.

### 3. Powertrain Layer

Models the interaction between the battery, power electronics, electric motor, and drivetrain to understand electrical and mechanical power flow.

### 4. Motor and Control Layer

Focuses on the electric motor and control system, including the relationship between motor speed, torque, power, and vehicle operation.

### 5. Vehicle Dynamics Layer

Represents vehicle-level behavior by considering speed, acceleration, torque requirements, power consumption, and driving conditions.

### 6. Simulation and Analysis Layer

Combines different models to simulate and analyze the complete electric vehicle system and evaluate its performance and energy efficiency.

### 7. Assessment Layer

Includes practical activities, assignments, quizzes, and assessments conducted throughout the internship.

## 🔄 Methodology

The internship followed a structured **week-wise learning methodology** that combined:

1. **Conceptual Learning** – Understanding the fundamental concepts of electric vehicles and simulation.
2. **MATLAB Fundamentals** – Learning MATLAB programming and engineering data analysis.
3. **Model-Based Development** – Creating and simulating models using Simulink.
4. **Physical-System Modeling** – Using Simscape for physical and electrical system simulation.
5. **Control and Logic Design** – Studying control systems, PID tuning, and Stateflow-based decision logic.
6. **Battery Modeling** – Analyzing battery systems, charging/discharging behavior, and battery management.
7. **Power Systems Simulation** – Modeling electrical power systems and power electronic components.
8. **System Architecture** – Understanding model-based systems engineering using System Composer.
9. **Application Development** – Building MATLAB applications using App Designer.
10. **Assessment and Evaluation** – Completing assignments, practical activities, quizzes, and assessments.
